Your media kit is the link in every pitch: your photo, your numbers with their dates, your best work, past brands and your rates. Nothing is public until you tap **Publish**.

## Open the Media kit tab

![Step 1](/help/screenshots/media-kit-1.png)
<!-- route: /deals -->
<!-- target: role=tab[name="Media kit"] -->

In **Deals**, tap **Media kit** at the top.

## Fix what the Kit check lists

![Step 2](/help/screenshots/media-kit-2.png)
<!-- target: .kit-check -->

The **Kit check** lists anything missing or old. Tap the button beside each one.

## Fill in your cover

![Step 3](/help/screenshots/media-kit-3.png)
<!-- target: #kit-handles -->

Photo, one line under your name, your niche, where you are and your handles. It saves as you type.

## Your numbers come from Stats

![Step 4](/help/screenshots/media-kit-4.png)
<!-- target: .kit-figs -->

Followers and views come from **Stats** with their dates. A number you type is marked self-reported.

## Pick 3 to 6 clips

![Step 5](/help/screenshots/media-kit-5.png)
<!-- target: #kit-showcase -->

Tick the clips brands should see.

## Preview, then Publish

![Step 6](/help/screenshots/media-kit-6.png)
<!-- target: role=button[name="Preview"] -->

Tap **Preview** to see what brands see, then **Publish**. Each publish is a dated version.

## Share the link or PDF

![Step 7](/help/screenshots/media-kit-7.png)
<!-- target: role=button[name="Copy link"] -->

**Copy link** for pitches, **Download PDF** for a printable copy. The bar says how often it was viewed.
