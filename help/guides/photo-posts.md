One photo becomes a few posts: a slow-zoom video, a photo set and three short captions (and a before-and-after video when you add a before and an after photo). They go out on their own. You get an email the day before, and you can skip any one.

## Add your photos

![Step 1](/help/screenshots/photo-posts-1.png)
<!-- target: role=button[name="Add photos from my phone"] -->

Open **Photos** and tap **Add photos from my phone**. JPG, PNG and iPhone photos all work.

## Have a before and after? Add them too

![Step 2](/help/screenshots/photo-posts-2.png)
<!-- target: role=button[name="Add the before photo"] -->

Tap **Add the before photo**, then **Add the after photo**. The studio makes a before-and-after video from them. This is optional.

## Tap Make my posts

![Step 3](/help/screenshots/photo-posts-3.png)
<!-- target: .photos-add [data-primary]:last-of-type -->

Write what the photo shows if you like (it helps the captions), then tap **Make my posts**. Faces and house numbers are blurred before anything posts.

## See what goes out, and when

![Step 4](/help/screenshots/photo-posts-4.png)
<!-- target: .photos-list .photos-card >> nth=0 -->

**Coming up** lists every post with its day and time. Videos the studio moved or made are labelled “Made with AI”.

## Skip one you do not want

![Step 5](/help/screenshots/photo-posts-5.png)
<!-- target: .photos-skip >> nth=0 -->

Tap **Skip this post**. It will not go out. Changed your mind? Tap **Undo skip**. The email the day before has a Skip link for each post too.

If not, tap **No** below and we'll email your helper.
