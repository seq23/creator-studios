Pinterest said the access token ran out or was changed. Your pins wait safely until it is connected again.

## Make a new access token

![Step 1](/help/screenshots/reconnect-pinterest-1.png)
<!-- target: #setup-pinterest .setup-where -->

On **Setup**, open the **Pinterest** card and follow the steps to make a new access token.

## Paste the new token

![Step 2](/help/screenshots/reconnect-pinterest-2.png)
<!-- target: #setup-pinterest .setup-form .field >> nth=0 -->

Paste it into **Access token**.

## Test it, then save

![Step 3](/help/screenshots/reconnect-pinterest-3.png)
<!-- target: #setup-pinterest .setup-actions -->

Tap **Test key**, then **Save**. Waiting pins are planned again, with a heads-up first.
