CapCut and InShot can't connect to other apps, so you send the clip there and upload your edit back. It replaces the clip; nothing else changes.

## Tap Edit in CapCut

![Step 1](/help/screenshots/edit-in-capcut-1.png)
<!-- target: [data-clip-id="demo_new_2"] >> text=Edit in CapCut -->

In **Review**, under the clip, tap **Edit in CapCut**.

## Pick your app

![Step 2](/help/screenshots/edit-in-capcut-2.png)
<!-- click: [data-clip-id="demo_new_2"] >> text=Edit in CapCut -->
<!-- target: .handoff-apps -->

Pick **CapCut**, **InShot** or **Another app**, then tap **Share** (phone) or **Save the clip** (computer).

## Open it in CapCut

![Step 3](/help/screenshots/edit-in-capcut-3.png)
<!-- mock: phone-share-sheet -->

Your phone's share sheet opens. Tap **CapCut**.

## Edit and export

![Step 4](/help/screenshots/edit-in-capcut-4.png)
<!-- mock: capcut-export -->

Make your changes, keep it tall (9:16), then tap **Export**.

## Tap Replace with my edit

![Step 5](/help/screenshots/edit-in-capcut-5.png)
<!-- click: [data-clip-id="demo_new_2"] >> text=Edit in CapCut -->
<!-- target: .handoff-steps -->

Back in the dashboard, tap **Replace with my edit** and pick your video. It's checked, the sound is levelled and a new cover is made in about a minute.
