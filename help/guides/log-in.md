{{studio}} has no password. Every time you log in on a new phone or computer, it emails you a 6-digit code.

## Open your studio

![Step 1](/help/screenshots/log-in-1.png)
<!-- target: .login-card -->

Open your studio's link on your phone or computer.

## Type your email

![Step 2](/help/screenshots/log-in-2.png)
<!-- target: input[type="email"] -->

Type your email and tap **Email me a code**.

## Find the code in your email

![Step 3](/help/screenshots/log-in-3.png)
<!-- mock: email-login-code -->

Open the email from {{studio}} and find the 6-digit code.

## Type the code

![Step 4](/help/screenshots/log-in-4.png)
<!-- fill: input[type="email"] => owner@studio.example -->
<!-- click: role=button[name="Email me a code"] -->
<!-- target: .code-input -->

Type the code and tap **Log in**. You stay logged in on that device for 30 days.
