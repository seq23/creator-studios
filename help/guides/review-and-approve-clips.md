## Open Review

![Step 1](/help/screenshots/review-and-approve-clips-1.png)
<!-- target: .review-tab.on -->

Open **Review**. The number beside **New** is how many clips are waiting.

## Watch a clip

![Step 2](/help/screenshots/review-and-approve-clips-2.png)
<!-- target: [data-clip-id="demo_new_1"] video -->

Clips are grouped by dump, best first. Tap play to watch.

## Tap Approve

![Step 3](/help/screenshots/review-and-approve-clips-3.png)
<!-- target: [data-clip-id="demo_new_1"] >> role=button[name="Approve"] -->

Like it? Tap **Approve**. Nothing posts unless you approve it.

## Or tap Reject and say why

![Step 4](/help/screenshots/review-and-approve-clips-4.png)
<!-- click: [data-clip-id="demo_new_2"] >> role=button[name="Reject"] -->
<!-- target: .reason-list -->

Not for you? Tap **Reject** and tap a reason. The reason teaches the cutter what you don't like.

## Approve all at once

![Step 5](/help/screenshots/review-and-approve-clips-5.png)
<!-- target: button:has-text("Approve all") -->

Happy with everything? Tap **Approve all** at the top, or tick a few clips and use the bar at the bottom. With more than 12 waiting it says **Approve these 12**: only the ones on your screen, never clips you have not seen.

## Add a voice over

![Step 6](/help/screenshots/review-and-approve-clips-6.png)
<!-- click: [data-clip-id="demo_new_1"] >> role=button[name="Add voice over"] -->
<!-- target: #v-script -->

**Add voice over** puts a short voice over in your voice on just this clip: change the words as you like, it fits the clip's length and is labelled as AI audio when it posts. A clip that already has one shows **Remove voice over** and **Redo voice over**.

## Changed your mind?

![Step 7](/help/screenshots/review-and-approve-clips-7.png)
<!-- target: .review-tab >> nth=2 -->

Open **Rejected** to bring a clip back. Rejected clips are removed after 7 days.

## See the lower-scoring clips

![Step 8](/help/screenshots/review-and-approve-clips-8.png)
<!-- target: label.switch:has-text("Show hidden") -->

Switch on **Show hidden** to see clips under the quality bar too.

**Try another version, with notes**: Tap **Try another version** on a clip. Leave the box empty to get the same moment in a new look, or write what should change (for example *Start with the best moment. Shorter.*) and we cut that video again with your notes. Your clip stays as it is until the new one is ready.

**Add a voice over to every clip in a dump**: Tap **Add voice over to all clips in this dump** under the dump's heading. Every clip with no talking gets a short voice over, in the voice you picked under **Settings → Sound**, with the music softly underneath. Each one is labelled **Made with AI** when it posts.

**Start over with the same videos**: Tap **Start over with these videos** under the dump's heading, then **Yes, start over**. The dump's new clips are removed; clips you approved or posted stay, and your videos stay. We cut the same videos again with your current settings (and anything you write in the box) and email you when they are ready.
