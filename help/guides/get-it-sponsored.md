Tell the studio an experience you want (a business studio: an event). It finds brands that sponsor it, with public proof, the videos to make and a dated to-do list. It's free, and you send every pitch yourself.

## Open it from Brand deals

![Step 1](/help/screenshots/get-it-sponsored-1.png)
<!-- target: [data-sponsor-card] -->

Open **Deals**. The card at the top says **Get an experience sponsored** (or **Get an event sponsored**). Tap it.

## Type what you want

![Step 2](/help/screenshots/get-it-sponsored-2.png)
<!-- route: /deals/sponsored -->
<!-- target: #sp-title -->

Type it in a few words, or tap an example. Pick **When** and **Who's coming**. Dates in your to-do list work back from the month.

## Tap Find brands for this

![Step 3](/help/screenshots/get-it-sponsored-3.png)
<!-- route: /deals/sponsored -->
<!-- target: role=button[name="Find brands for this"] -->

It takes 3–6 minutes. You can leave the page; it keeps going and shows under **Your experiences** when it's ready.

## A treatment shows its rules first

![Step 4](/help/screenshots/get-it-sponsored-4.png)
<!-- route: /deals/sponsored/demo_spn_med -->
<!-- target: section[aria-label="Read before you book"] -->

Anything medical or cosmetic (Botox, a clinic, surgery) shows **Read before you book** above the brands: label it #ad, no medical claims, a licensed provider, and the platform's own rules.

## Pitch a brand

![Step 5](/help/screenshots/get-it-sponsored-5.png)
<!-- route: /deals/sponsored/demo_spn_trip -->
<!-- target: role=button[name="Pitch this brand"] >> nth=0 -->

Each brand says why it fits, links the public page that proves it works with creators, and its public way in. Tap **Pitch this brand**: it goes into your Deals with the first email written. You send it yourself. **Not for me** hides it; you can bring it back.

## Add a video to your Calendar

![Step 6](/help/screenshots/get-it-sponsored-6.png)
<!-- route: /deals/sponsored/demo_spn_trip -->
<!-- click: role=tab[name=/Videos/] -->
<!-- target: role=button[name="Add to my Calendar"] >> nth=0 -->

On **Videos**, each idea has its hook and shots. Tap **Add to my Calendar** and pick the day you'll film it. It shows on Calendar under **To film**; nothing posts.

## Tick off your to-do list

![Step 7](/help/screenshots/get-it-sponsored-7.png)
<!-- route: /deals/sponsored/demo_spn_trip -->
<!-- click: role=tab[name=/To do/] -->
<!-- target: section[aria-label="To do"] -->

**To do** has every step with its date. Tick each one as you go; ticks are saved, even when you tap **Run it again**.
