YouTube lets apps post only Shorts (vertical, 3 minutes or less). A landscape or longer video, or one Buffer couldn't take, goes up from your own YouTube account in two taps.

## Download it

![Step 1](/help/screenshots/upload-it-yourself-1.png)
<!-- route: /review?tab=approved -->
<!-- target: [data-clip-id="demo_full_3"] [data-handoff] a:has-text("Download for YouTube") -->

In **Review → Approved**, tap **Download for YouTube** on the video. Home shows the same button when it is time.

## Upload it on YouTube

![Step 2](/help/screenshots/upload-it-yourself-2.png)
<!-- mock: youtube-upload -->

Tap **Open YouTube upload**, pick the video, paste the title and description from the dashboard, pick who can see it, and publish.

## We mark it posted

![Step 3](/help/screenshots/upload-it-yourself-3.png)
<!-- route: /review?tab=approved -->
<!-- target: [data-clip-id="demo_full_3"] [data-handoff] -->

When it shows on your channel, the dashboard finds it by its title and marks it **Posted** with its link. Nothing else to do.

## Or paste its link

![Step 4](/help/screenshots/upload-it-yourself-4.png)
<!-- route: /review?tab=approved -->
<!-- fill: [data-clip-id="demo_full_3"] [data-handoff] input => https://youtu.be/demoyt00004 -->

Want it marked now? Paste the video's link and tap **Mark posted**.
