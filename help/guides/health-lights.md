Green: working. Yellow: needs a look soon. Red: not working, and it says how to fix it. Grey: not set up (fine for optional things).

## Home shows the worst first

![Step 1](/help/screenshots/health-lights-1.png)
<!-- route: / -->
<!-- target: .home-health -->

On **Home**, the **Health** card lists the lights, worst first.

## See every light

![Step 2](/help/screenshots/health-lights-2.png)
<!-- target: section[aria-label="Connections and health"] -->

Open **Settings**, **Connections + health**.

## A red light: tap How to fix

![Step 3](/help/screenshots/health-lights-3.png)
<!-- target: [data-health="Instagram (via Buffer)"] >> text=How to fix -->

Every red or yellow light has **How to fix**, which opens its picture guide.

## A yellow light

![Step 4](/help/screenshots/health-lights-4.png)
<!-- target: [data-health="Storage"] -->

Yellow means soon, not now. This one fixes itself as old footage clears.

## Fixed? It turns green

![Step 5](/help/screenshots/health-lights-5.png)
<!-- light: Instagram (via Buffer) | green | Posting OK -->
<!-- target: [data-health="Instagram (via Buffer)"] -->

After fixing something, tap **Check everything now**. The light turns green.
