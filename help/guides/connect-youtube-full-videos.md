Do this once. Your full videos then upload straight to your own YouTube channel with the thumbnail, title, description, chapters and tags you approved, at the time on your Calendar. Your Shorts, TikToks and Reels keep going through Buffer.

## Tap Connect YouTube (full videos)

![Step 1](/help/screenshots/connect-youtube-full-videos-1.png)
<!-- route: /settings/connections -->
<!-- target: [data-youtube-direct] a[href="/api/oauth/youtube/start"] -->

On **Settings**, **Connect accounts**, under **YouTube · full videos**, tap **Connect YouTube (full videos)**.

## Pick your Google account

![Step 2](/help/screenshots/connect-youtube-full-videos-2.png)
<!-- mock: youtube-account -->

Pick the Google account your YouTube channel is on.

## Tap the small Advanced link

![Step 3](/help/screenshots/connect-youtube-full-videos-3.png)
<!-- mock: youtube-unverified -->

Google shows **Google hasn't verified this app**. That is expected: it's your own dashboard, not an app Google has reviewed. Don't tap Back to safety; tap the small **Advanced** link.

## Tap Go to workers.dev (unsafe)

![Step 4](/help/screenshots/connect-youtube-full-videos-4.png)
<!-- mock: youtube-unsafe -->

Tap **Go to workers.dev (unsafe)**. Google says "unsafe" only because it hasn't reviewed the app; workers.dev is where your dashboard lives.

## Tap Continue

![Step 5](/help/screenshots/connect-youtube-full-videos-5.png)
<!-- mock: youtube-allow -->

Leave both boxes ticked and tap **Continue**. Google words the second box "edit and delete"; the dashboard only moves and hides your videos, never deletes one. You're back on Connect with a green light. That's all: nothing else to do, ever.

## Did this work?

If the light is red, tap **Reconnect YouTube**. If YouTube asks you to verify your channel for custom thumbnails, open youtube.com/verify and add your phone number; your videos still go up meanwhile.
