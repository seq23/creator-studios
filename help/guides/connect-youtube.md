One tap on Connect, then Google's own page. The long version with pictures of Google's pages is **Connect YouTube for your full videos**.

## Find YouTube · full videos on Connect

![Step 1](/help/screenshots/connect-youtube-1.png)
<!-- route: /settings/connections -->
<!-- target: [data-youtube-direct] -->

On **Settings**, **Connect accounts**, the second box is **YouTube · full videos**. Tap **Connect YouTube (full videos)**.

## Advanced, Go to workers.dev (unsafe), Continue

![Step 2](/help/screenshots/connect-youtube-2.png)
<!-- route: /settings/connections -->
<!-- target: [data-youtube-direct] [data-unverified-note] -->

Pick your account. Google says it hasn't verified the app: tap the small **Advanced** link, then **Go to workers.dev (unsafe)**, then **Continue**. The long guide has a picture of each page.

## Check the light

![Step 3](/help/screenshots/connect-youtube-3.png)
<!-- route: /settings/connections -->
<!-- target: [data-youtube-direct] .meta -->

Back on **Connect**, the line under **YouTube (full videos)** says **Connected** with your channel's name and the light is green. Nothing else to do.
