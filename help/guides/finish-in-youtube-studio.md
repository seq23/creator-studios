Buffer posts your full video but can't send its custom thumbnail or its tags. Those two take a minute in YouTube Studio.

## Home tells you

![Step 1](/help/screenshots/finish-in-youtube-studio-1.png)
<!-- route: / -->
<!-- target: [data-youtube-card="finish_in_studio"] -->

After it posts, Home says **is up. Add its thumbnail and tags** with the links you need.

## Get the thumbnail and tags

![Step 2](/help/screenshots/finish-in-youtube-studio-2.png)
<!-- route: /review?tab=approved -->
<!-- target: [data-clip-id="demo_full_2"] [data-finish-studio] .btn-row -->

In **Review → Approved**, tap **Download thumbnail** and **Copy tags**, then **Open YouTube Studio**.

## Set them in YouTube Studio

![Step 3](/help/screenshots/finish-in-youtube-studio-3.png)
<!-- mock: youtube-studio-details -->

On the video's page, upload the thumbnail you downloaded, paste the tags, and tap **Save**.

## Tap I did it

![Step 4](/help/screenshots/finish-in-youtube-studio-4.png)
<!-- route: /review?tab=approved -->
<!-- target: [data-clip-id="demo_full_2"] [data-finish-studio] button:has-text("I did it") -->

Back in Review, tap **I did it** and the Home card goes away.
