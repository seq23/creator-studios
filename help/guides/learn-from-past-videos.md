Show the studio 3 to 10 of your finished videos that did well. It watches them and writes down how your videos feel, so new clips feel like yours. They are never cut into clips.

## Choose your past videos

![Step 1](/help/screenshots/learn-from-past-videos-1.png)
<!-- route: /brain -->
<!-- target: role=button[name="Choose past videos"] -->

Open **Client Brain**, scroll to **My past videos** and tap **Choose past videos**. Pick 3 to 10 finished videos.

## Press Watch my videos

![Step 2](/help/screenshots/learn-from-past-videos-2.png)
<!-- route: /brain -->
<!-- target: [data-past-videos] p.soft -->

When 3 are in, tap **Watch my videos**. It takes 10 to 20 minutes; you can leave the page.

## Read it, change it, lock it

![Step 3](/help/screenshots/learn-from-past-videos-3.png)
<!-- route: /brain -->
<!-- target: #sec-video_feel -->

**How my videos feel** appears in your Brand Profile: hooks, pace, length, captions, music, how you talk to camera. Change anything, then tap **Lock profile**. Until you lock it, your clips keep using your last locked profile.

If not, tap **No** below and we'll open the matching fix-it guide or email your helper.
