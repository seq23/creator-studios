Clips where you don't talk (the table, the flowers, the reveal) can get a short voice over in your own voice, made for you after each dump. Clips where you talk never get one.

## Switch it on (or off)

![Step 1](/help/screenshots/automatic-voice-overs-1.png)
<!-- route: /voice -->
<!-- target: label.switch:has-text("Automatic voice overs") -->

On **Voice overs**, **Automatic voice overs** is on unless you switched it off. It needs your recorded voice first.

## Choose for one dump

![Step 2](/help/screenshots/automatic-voice-overs-2.png)
<!-- route: /dump -->
<!-- click: [data-door="new"] -->
<!-- target: [role="group"][aria-label="Voice over"] -->

On **Dump**, the **Voice over** choice decides for this dump only: on quiet clips, none, or let me pick in Review. A note like "no voice over" works too.

## See it in Review

![Step 3](/help/screenshots/automatic-voice-overs-3.png)
<!-- route: /review?tab=approved -->
<!-- target: [data-clip-id="demo_clip_3"] [data-voice-over="ready"] -->

A clip with a voice over says **With your voice over · added automatically**. Tap play to hear it.

## Change the words, or remove it

![Step 4](/help/screenshots/automatic-voice-overs-4.png)
<!-- route: /review?tab=approved -->
<!-- target: [data-clip-id="demo_clip_3"] [aria-label="Voice over"] -->

**Redo voice over** opens **Edit the script**: change the words and it is made again in your voice. **Remove voice over** puts the clip back as it was.

## It is labelled when it posts

![Step 5](/help/screenshots/automatic-voice-overs-5.png)
<!-- route: /voice -->
<!-- target: .clips-switch .switch-hint -->

Your voice over is made by AI from your recording, so the post goes out marked as AI audio on TikTok, Instagram and YouTube.
