Hugging Face said the token ran out or was changed. Photo videos use the slow zoom until it is connected again, so nothing waits.

## Make a new token

![Step 1](/help/screenshots/reconnect-huggingface-1.png)
<!-- target: #setup-huggingface .setup-where -->

On **Setup**, open the **Connect Hugging Face (free) for realistic motion** card and follow the steps to make a new token.

## Paste the new token

![Step 2](/help/screenshots/reconnect-huggingface-2.png)
<!-- target: #setup-huggingface .setup-form .field >> nth=0 -->

Paste it into **API key**.

## Test it, then save

![Step 3](/help/screenshots/reconnect-huggingface-3.png)
<!-- target: #setup-huggingface .setup-actions -->

Tap **Test key**, then **Save**. The next photo video moves realistically again.
