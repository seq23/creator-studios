The dashboard writes the email; you send it yourself from your own Gmail.

## Tap Pitch

![Step 1](/help/screenshots/pitch-a-brand-1.png)
<!-- target: role=button[name="Pitch"] >> nth=0 -->

On **Deals**, tap **Pitch** on a brand. The deal opens with your first email written.

## Pick the tone and length

![Step 2](/help/screenshots/pitch-a-brand-2.png)
<!-- route: /deals?deal=demo_deal_2 -->
<!-- target: .email-controls -->

Try **Warm**, **Straight** or **Short**, and a length. Tap **Rewrite** to write it again.

## Pick a subject line

![Step 3](/help/screenshots/pitch-a-brand-3.png)
<!-- route: /deals?deal=demo_deal_2 -->
<!-- target: .email-subjects -->

Three subject lines to choose from.

## Tap Open in Gmail

![Step 4](/help/screenshots/pitch-a-brand-4.png)
<!-- route: /deals?deal=demo_deal_2 -->
<!-- target: role=link[name="Open in Gmail"] -->

Gmail opens with the address, subject and email filled in.

## Press Send in Gmail

![Step 5](/help/screenshots/pitch-a-brand-5.png)
<!-- mock: gmail-send -->

Read it once more and press **Send**.

## Come back and tap Mark sent

![Step 6](/help/screenshots/pitch-a-brand-6.png)
<!-- route: /deals?deal=demo_deal_2 -->
<!-- target: role=button[name="Mark sent"] -->

Tap **Mark sent**. Follow-ups come up on day 5, 12 and 19, each written for you.
