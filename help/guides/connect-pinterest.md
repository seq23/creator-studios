Your photo sets are pinned to your Pinterest board once it is connected. Until then they wait safely on Photos.

## Find Pinterest on Setup

![Step 1](/help/screenshots/connect-pinterest-1.png)
<!-- target: #setup-pinterest -->

Open **Setup** and find the **Pinterest** card.

## Get an access token

![Step 2](/help/screenshots/connect-pinterest-2.png)
<!-- target: #setup-pinterest a.btn -->

Tap **Open Pinterest developers**, connect an app, and make a token with **pins:write** and **boards:read**.

## Paste it and save

![Step 3](/help/screenshots/connect-pinterest-3.png)
<!-- target: #setup-pinterest button[type="submit"] -->

Paste the token and your board's id (the number in the board's link), then tap **Save**.
