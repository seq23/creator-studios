When a video shows a TikTok or Instagram name that isn't one of your accounts, its clips are held off your calendar.

## Read the note

![Step 1](/help/screenshots/someone-elses-video-1.png)
<!-- target: [data-held-note] -->

On **Dump** (or in **Review**), the note says which name was seen.

## It is yours? Tap This is my video

![Step 2](/help/screenshots/someone-elses-video-2.png)
<!-- target: role=button[name="This is my video"] -->

An old post of yours or an account you run: tap **This is my video**. Its clips can go on the calendar.

## Not yours? Reject its clips

![Step 3](/help/screenshots/someone-elses-video-3.png)
<!-- route: /review -->
<!-- target: [data-clip-id="demo_held_1"] >> role=button[name="Reject"] -->

Posting another creator's video can get your account flagged. Reject its clips in **Review**.
