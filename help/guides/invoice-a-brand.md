After you agree, the dashboard runs the rest like a manager: delivery, results, invoice, reminders.

## Tick off delivery

![Step 1](/help/screenshots/invoice-a-brand-1.png)
<!-- route: /deals?deal=demo_deal_5 -->
<!-- target: .delivery-card -->

In **Delivery**, tick each step as it happens: brief, idea, draft, approved, posted with #ad.

## Make the invoice

![Step 2](/help/screenshots/invoice-a-brand-2.png)
<!-- route: /deals?deal=demo_deal_6 -->
<!-- target: button:has-text("Open invoice"), a:has-text("Open invoice") -->

When it's posted, tap **Make the invoice**, then **Open invoice** and save it as a PDF.

## Send it and wait

![Step 3](/help/screenshots/invoice-a-brand-3.png)
<!-- route: /deals?deal=demo_deal_6 -->
<!-- target: .email-panel -->

Send the invoice email with the PDF. If payment is late, a polite reminder is written for you.

## Tap Mark paid

![Step 4](/help/screenshots/invoice-a-brand-4.png)
<!-- route: /deals?deal=demo_deal_6 -->
<!-- target: role=button[name="Mark paid"] -->

When the money lands, tap **Mark paid**. The brand is added to your media kit's past work.
