You never post by hand. You approve clips, the Calendar fills your week, and Buffer publishes each one at its time.

## Approve the clips you like

![Step 1](/help/screenshots/how-posting-works-1.png)
<!-- route: /review -->
<!-- target: [data-clip-id="demo_new_3"] >> role=button[name="Approve"] -->

In **Review**, tap **Approve**. Only approved clips can post.

## The Calendar fills itself

![Step 2](/help/screenshots/how-posting-works-2.png)
<!-- route: /calendar -->
<!-- target: role=button[name="Fill the calendar"] -->

Approved clips fill your week every hour. Tap **Fill the calendar** to do it now.

## Buffer takes it from there

![Step 3](/help/screenshots/how-posting-works-3.png)
<!-- route: /calendar -->
<!-- target: [data-post="demo_post_h3"] -->

About a day before, each post goes to Buffer, which publishes it at its time.

## Posted, with its link

![Step 4](/help/screenshots/how-posting-works-4.png)
<!-- route: /calendar -->
<!-- target: [data-post="demo_post_h1"] -->

Once it's live the post says so, with a link to see it.

## Each app has a light

![Step 5](/help/screenshots/how-posting-works-5.png)
<!-- route: /settings -->
<!-- target: [data-health="Instagram (via Buffer)"] -->

If an app's light is red, its posts wait safely until you fix it.
