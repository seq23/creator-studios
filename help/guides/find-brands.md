Every day the dashboard looks for brands with money for creators like you, and ranks them. It needs no key; a Firecrawl key only makes it faster.

## Brands to pitch this week

![Step 1](/help/screenshots/find-brands-1.png)
<!-- target: section[aria-label="Brands to pitch this week"] -->

Open **Deals**. The top cards already pay creators; each says how we know, with the page it came from.

## See how it ranked

![Step 2](/help/screenshots/find-brands-2.png)
<!-- target: summary:has-text("How it ranked") >> nth=0 -->

Tap **How it ranked** for the math: budget, fit and how easy they are to reach.

## Tap Find brands now

![Step 3](/help/screenshots/find-brands-3.png)
<!-- target: role=button[name="Find brands now"] -->

New brands come every day on their own. Tap **Find brands now** for a fresh search.

## Add a brand you love

![Step 4](/help/screenshots/find-brands-4.png)
<!-- target: role=button[name="Add a brand I love"] -->

Tap **Add a brand I love** to add one yourself.

## Not a fit? Say so

![Step 5](/help/screenshots/find-brands-5.png)
<!-- target: role=button[name="Not a fit"] >> nth=0 -->

Tap **Not a fit** and it won't come back.
