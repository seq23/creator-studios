Your clips post to Instagram, TikTok and YouTube through Buffer, a free posting service. Here is why, in plain words.

## Why not post straight to Instagram and TikTok?

![Step 1](/help/screenshots/why-we-post-through-buffer-1.png)
<!-- route: /settings/connections -->
<!-- target: .card:has(h3:text-is("Buffer")) [data-why-buffer] -->

Instagram and TikTok only let an app post for you after it passes their own review, which takes weeks to months. Buffer already passed it, so your clips can post today.

## What it costs, and your passwords

![Step 2](/help/screenshots/why-we-post-through-buffer-2.png)
<!-- route: /settings/connections -->
<!-- target: .card:has(h3:text-is("Buffer")) p.soft -->

Buffer's free plan covers 3 channels: Instagram, TikTok and YouTube. You sign in on Instagram's and TikTok's own pages inside Buffer; we never see your passwords. You can disconnect any time.

## If we get direct approval

![Step 3](/help/screenshots/why-we-post-through-buffer-3.png)
<!-- route: /settings/connections -->
<!-- target: role=heading[name="Connect your accounts"] -->

We'll switch you over ourselves, with nothing for you to redo. Until then, **Connect Instagram & TikTok** in the menu opens this page and walks you through Buffer.
