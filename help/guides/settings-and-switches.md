Every feature starts switched on, so you can use anything you like. Turn off what you don't want; nothing is ever hidden.

## Features

![Step 1](/help/screenshots/settings-and-switches-1.png)
<!-- route: /settings -->
<!-- target: section:has(h2:text-is("Features")) -->

Open **Settings** and find **Features**. Each switch says what off and on mean.

## Automatic voice overs

![Step 2](/help/screenshots/settings-and-switches-2.png)
<!-- route: /settings -->
<!-- target: label.switch:has-text("Automatic voice overs") -->

On: clips with no talking get a voice over in your voice automatically, and you can remove it in Review. Off: only the voice overs you add yourself.

## Deeper web research

![Step 3](/help/screenshots/settings-and-switches-3.png)
<!-- route: /settings -->
<!-- target: label.switch:has-text("Deeper web research") -->

On lets the research search more of the web. It's free either way.

## Weekly recap email

![Step 4](/help/screenshots/settings-and-switches-4.png)
<!-- route: /settings -->
<!-- target: label.switch:has-text("Weekly recap email") -->

Every Monday: last week's posts, what worked and your follow-ups.

## Music bed

![Step 5](/help/screenshots/settings-and-switches-5.png)
<!-- route: /settings -->
<!-- target: label.switch:has-text("Music bed") -->

Plays your own song quietly under clips. It turns on when you add a song under **My music**.

Under **Sound**:

**Camera sound**: **Automatic** keeps someone talking to the camera and drops background talk or noise; **Keep it** keeps every clip's own sound; **Off, music instead** drops it, with music under every clip (your songs, or the free music that comes with your studio) and no captions from what the camera heard. You can change it for one dump with the **Camera sound** choice on the Dump screen.

**Voice for voice overs**: **A studio voice** is a free voice that comes with your studio (tap play to listen, then **Use this voice**); **My recorded voice** uses the recording on **Voice overs**. Voice overs are always labelled **Made with AI** when they post.
