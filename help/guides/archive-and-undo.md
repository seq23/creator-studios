After a few months, finished dumps, closed deals and old notices pile up. One tap moves each one out of the way, and one tap brings it back. Nothing is deleted.

## Hide a card on Home

![Step 1](/help/screenshots/archive-and-undo-1.png)
<!-- route: / -->
<!-- target: button[aria-label="Hide this notice"] -->

Tap the **×** on a Home card you have dealt with. The next one takes its place, and **Undo** in the message at the bottom brings it straight back. Home always fits on one phone screen; **See all** opens the rest.

## Archive a dump you are done with

![Step 2](/help/screenshots/archive-and-undo-2.png)
<!-- route: /dump -->
<!-- target: [data-dump-row] .dismiss-btn >> nth=0 -->

On **Dump**, tap the **×** on a dump. It moves to Archived; its clips stay exactly where they are.

## Find it again

![Step 3](/help/screenshots/archive-and-undo-3.png)
<!-- route: /dump -->
<!-- target: [data-show-archived] -->

Tap **Show archived**, then **Restore** on the one you want back. Deals, voice overs and research briefs work the same way.

## Archive a deal that is over

![Step 4](/help/screenshots/archive-and-undo-4.png)
<!-- route: /deals -->
<!-- target: .deal-card-wrap .dismiss-btn >> nth=0 -->

In **Deals**, tap the **×** on a deal card, or **Archive** inside a deal. Money you are owed never leaves on its own: an invoiced deal stays until it is paid.

## Let Tidy up do it for you

![Step 5](/help/screenshots/archive-and-undo-5.png)
<!-- route: /settings -->
<!-- target: #tidy -->

**Settings → Tidy up** archives finished things on its own: dumps 30 days after you finish them, deals 60 days after they close, quiet deals after 90 days, replaced research briefs after 90 days, voice overs that failed after 14 days. Switch it off any time.
