Voice overs are spoken in your own voice. They are optional; your clips stay real footage either way. Recording takes about 3 minutes.

## Open Voice overs

![Step 1](/help/screenshots/record-your-voice-1.png)
<!-- route: / -->
<!-- api: DELETE /api/voice/sample -->
<!-- target: .home-voice -->

On **Home**, tap **Set up in 5 steps** on **Your voice overs** (or open **Voice overs** in the menu).

## Find a quiet spot

![Step 2](/help/screenshots/record-your-voice-2.png)
<!-- route: /voice -->
<!-- target: .voice-step[data-step="1"] -->

Somewhere quiet. Hold your phone like a call, a hand's width from your mouth.

## Record while you read

![Step 3](/help/screenshots/record-your-voice-3.png)
<!-- route: /voice -->
<!-- target: .rec-btn -->

Tap the red **Record** button and read the script on screen out loud. Mistakes are fine; keep going. At least 1 minute.

## Listen back

![Step 4](/help/screenshots/record-your-voice-4.png)
<!-- route: /voice -->
<!-- target: .voice-step[data-step="3"] -->

Tap **Stop** and play it back. Used your phone's Voice Memos app instead? Tap **Upload a recording**.

## Say yes and save

![Step 5](/help/screenshots/record-your-voice-5.png)
<!-- route: /voice -->
<!-- target: .consent -->

Tick **This is my own voice and I consent**, then tap **Save my voice**. The built-in voice is ready in a few minutes.

## Voice overs on quiet clips, automatically

![Step 6](/help/screenshots/record-your-voice-6.png)
<!-- route: /voice -->
<!-- target: .clips-switch -->

With your voice saved, **Automatic voice overs** gives clips where you don't talk a short voice over in your voice after each dump. See **Automatic voice overs on quiet clips**.

## Re-record any time

![Step 7](/help/screenshots/record-your-voice-7.png)
<!-- route: /voice -->
<!-- target: .voice-step[data-step="5"] -->

The newest recording replaces the old one. **Delete my voice** removes it for good.
