Every dump keeps a list of the photos and videos you sent, what the studio saw in each one, and which clips used it.

## Open what you dumped

![Step 1](/help/screenshots/what-i-dumped-1.png)
<!-- route: /review -->
<!-- target: [data-files-link] >> nth=0 -->

In **Review**, under each dump's date, tap **See what you dumped**. The dump's own page has the same link once its clips are ready.

## See every file

![Step 2](/help/screenshots/what-i-dumped-2.png)
<!-- route: /dump/demo_dump_2/files -->
<!-- target: [data-files-summary] -->

The top line counts your videos and photos, says how many are not in a clip yet, and the day the originals are cleared to save space. Your clips stay.

## See which clips used each file

![Step 3](/help/screenshots/what-i-dumped-3.png)
<!-- route: /dump/demo_dump_2/files -->
<!-- target: [data-file-id] >> nth=1 -->

Each file shows what the studio saw in it and **Used in** with the clip numbers, like **Clip 2 (ends on it)**. Tap a number to open that clip in Review, or tap the picture to look at the file.
