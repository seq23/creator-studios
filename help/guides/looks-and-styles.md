Every clip is made in a **look**: its captions, its layout and a little movement. Each dump takes turns through the looks so your clips never all look the same. It's all free.

## See each clip's look

![Step 1](/help/screenshots/looks-and-styles-1.png)
<!-- target: [data-clip-id="demo_new_1"] .look-chip -->

In **Review**, each clip says **Look:** and its name.

## Tap Change look

![Step 2](/help/screenshots/looks-and-styles-2.png)
<!-- click: [data-clip-id="demo_new_1"] >> text=Change look -->
<!-- target: .look-list -->

Under the clip, tap **Change look**. Every look has a small preview and one line on what it does.

## Pick a look

![Step 3](/help/screenshots/looks-and-styles-3.png)
<!-- click: [data-clip-id="demo_new_1"] >> text=Change look -->
<!-- target: [data-look-option="karaoke"] -->

Tap the one you want. Only this clip is made again, in about a minute; the old one plays until the new one is ready.

## Choose which looks are in the mix

![Step 4](/help/screenshots/looks-and-styles-4.png)
<!-- route: /settings -->
<!-- target: label.editing-look >> nth=0 -->

Open **Settings**, then **Editing**. Every look is on to start; untick any you never want.

## Captions and your end card

![Step 5](/help/screenshots/looks-and-styles-5.png)
<!-- route: /settings -->
<!-- target: label.switch:has-text("End card") -->

Switch captions or your end card (logo and handle) on or off here.

## Add your own music

![Step 6](/help/screenshots/looks-and-styles-6.png)
<!-- route: /settings -->
<!-- target: label:has-text("Add a song") -->

Under **My music**, tap **Add a song** and pick a song you own. Only your own songs are used, so no post gets muted.
