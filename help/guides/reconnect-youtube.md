Google stopped accepting the dashboard's sign-in (you removed its access, or Google asked again). Nothing is lost: videos already on your channel stay there, and new ones wait on Home under **Upload it yourself** until you reconnect.

## Tap Reconnect YouTube

![Step 1](/help/screenshots/reconnect-youtube-1.png)
<!-- route: /settings/connections -->
<!-- target: [data-youtube-direct] .title -->

On **Settings**, **Connect accounts**, under **YouTube · full videos**, tap **Reconnect YouTube** (the light is red).

## Tap Advanced, then Go to workers.dev (unsafe)

![Step 2](/help/screenshots/reconnect-youtube-2.png)
<!-- mock: youtube-unsafe -->
<!-- shared -->

Pick your account. On **Google hasn't verified this app**, tap the small **Advanced** link, then **Go to workers.dev (unsafe)**. It's your own dashboard.

## Tap Continue

![Step 3](/help/screenshots/reconnect-youtube-3.png)
<!-- mock: youtube-allow -->
<!-- shared -->

Leave both boxes ticked and tap **Continue** (the dashboard never deletes a video). The light turns green and waiting videos upload within the hour.
