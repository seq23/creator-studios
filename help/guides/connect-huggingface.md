One photo can become a short video where the camera moves gently, made by a free open-source AI model. Until Hugging Face is connected, photo videos use the slow zoom, and everything else works as usual. Videos made this way are labelled “Made with AI”.

## Find Hugging Face on Setup

![Step 1](/help/screenshots/connect-huggingface-1.png)
<!-- target: #setup-huggingface -->

Open **Setup** and find the **Connect Hugging Face (free) for realistic motion** card.

## Make a free token

![Step 2](/help/screenshots/connect-huggingface-2.png)
<!-- target: #setup-huggingface a.btn -->

Tap **Open Hugging Face tokens**, sign in (a free account is enough), tap **Create new token**, pick **Read**, and copy it.

## Paste it and save

![Step 3](/help/screenshots/connect-huggingface-3.png)
<!-- target: #setup-huggingface button[type="submit"] -->

Paste the token, then tap **Save**. If the free model is busy, the studio uses the slow zoom for that video instead, so nothing waits.
