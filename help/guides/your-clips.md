Every clip ends by asking people to do one thing and sends them to one link. You choose both here, plus how many clips each dump gives and how good a clip must be to show.

## Open Your clips in Settings

![Step 1](/help/screenshots/your-clips-1.png)
<!-- target: #your-clips h2 -->

Open **Settings** and find **Your clips**. Everything here changes the next clips you dump, never the ones already made.

## Say what clips ask, and where they send people

![Step 2](/help/screenshots/your-clips-2.png)
<!-- target: #cta-link -->

Type a few short asks, one per line, then paste the page people should land on and tap **Save the link**. Each platform gets its own tracking version of the link, so you can see where people came from.

## Choose when a caption says "Made with AI"

![Step 3](/help/screenshots/your-clips-3.png)
<!-- target: [aria-label="When a caption says Made with AI"] -->

Pick **Every clip**, or **Only clips with an AI picture** (a photo with no camera details). The note sits on its own line at the end of the caption, never on the video.

## Set how many clips and the review bar

![Step 4](/help/screenshots/your-clips-4.png)
<!-- target: [aria-label="Review bar"] -->

Use **−** and **+** for how many clips a dump gives and for the review bar. A clip under the bar is made again once; if it is still under, it waits under **Show hidden** in Review with the reason.
