Each package has a public **Starting at** price and a private target and floor only you see. Suggestions show their math and source, or say there's no benchmark.

## Packages and rates

![Step 1](/help/screenshots/set-your-rates-1.png)
<!-- target: #kit-packages -->

On **Deals**, **Media kit**, find **Packages and rates**.

## Tap Suggest prices

![Step 2](/help/screenshots/set-your-rates-2.png)
<!-- target: role=button[name="Suggest prices"] -->

In the **Kit check**, tap **Suggest prices** for starting prices from your own deals or a named 2026 benchmark.

## Use a suggestion

![Step 3](/help/screenshots/set-your-rates-3.png)
<!-- target: role=button[name="Use these"] >> nth=0 -->

Each package shows its suggestion and source. Tap **Use these** to take it.

## Your private floor

![Step 4](/help/screenshots/set-your-rates-4.png)
<!-- target: label.field:has-text("Your floor") >> nth=0 -->

**Your floor** is the lowest you'd take. Brands never see it; the negotiation helper uses it.

## Add-ons and terms

![Step 5](/help/screenshots/set-your-rates-5.png)
<!-- target: h3:text-is("Add-ons and terms") -->

Usage, paid ads, exclusivity and payment terms, each with its published range.
