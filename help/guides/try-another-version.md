Not quite right? Make just this clip again, a different way or with different music. The old one plays until the new one is ready.

## Tap Try another version

![Step 1](/help/screenshots/try-another-version-1.png)
<!-- target: [data-clip-id="demo_new_2"] >> text=Try another version -->

Under the clip, tap **Try another version** for a fresh take on the same moment.

## Tap Change music

![Step 2](/help/screenshots/try-another-version-2.png)
<!-- target: [data-clip-id="demo_new_2"] >> text=Change music -->

Tap **Change music** to pick a song for this clip.

## Pick a song, or none

![Step 3](/help/screenshots/try-another-version-3.png)
<!-- click: [data-clip-id="demo_new_2"] >> text=Change music -->
<!-- target: role=dialog[name="Change music"] >> .reason-list -->

Only your own songs from **My music** are used. It's ready in about a minute.
